Website features without tracking

The portfolio pages themselves use no cookies, local storage or session storage. I use no analytics or advertising trackers, externally loaded fonts, contact forms or embedded social media or video content. Images, fonts and other page elements are served with the website.

Animations and the rotating Sumi captions run in your browser. These interactions are not sent to me. The website does not create usage profiles. You only access another website when you open an external link.

Domain hannahreber.de

The domain hannahreber.de is registered with united-domains GmbH in Starnberg, Germany, and connected directly to GitHub Pages via DNS. The portfolio pages are served by GitHub Pages; no web forwarding through united-domains takes place.

Hosting on GitHub Pages

This portfolio is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives connection and request data for this purpose, particularly your IP address. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, even when visitors are not signed in.

To the extent that I am responsible for this processing, it is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR and my interest in securely providing my professional portfolio.

GitHub describes processing in the United States and other countries and cites EU Standard Contractual Clauses and its participation in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework as safeguards. Its privacy notice explains its general retention criteria and contact options. It does not state a fixed retention period for Pages access logs.

Contact by email

The email button opens your email application; this alone does not send a message. If you write to me, I process your sender address, the message content and any information or attachments you choose to include so that I can address your enquiry and reply.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is corresponding about professional enquiries and my portfolio. My mailbox is provided by united-domains GmbH in Starnberg, Germany. The provider processes messages for transmission and storage and states that its mailboxes are hosted in Germany.

I delete messages once your enquiry has been resolved and they are no longer needed for further correspondence. Statutory retention obligations or the need to protect legal claims may justify longer retention. You decide which information to send; without the necessary contact details and information about your enquiry, I may be unable to reply.

Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. To exercise these rights, contact me at the email address given above.

Where I process data on the basis of Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the place where you live or work or where you believe an infringement has occurred. In Berlin, the competent authority is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
